Watches: screens for approval

Watches gets its own tab: Chat · Today · Money · Watches · More. Jarvis checks bus fares (redBus), movie bookings (BookMyShow), rides (BlaBlaCar) and any web page on a schedule, and tells you when something good happens. The AI only helps when you create a watch; every check after that is a simple, quick script. Every watch you create is kept: when its date passes it moves to Saved, and you can start it again for a new day whenever you want. All prices and names here are examples.
