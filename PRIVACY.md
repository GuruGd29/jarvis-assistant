# Privacy policy: Jarvis

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant that one person runs for themselves on their own computer or server.
It is not a service offered to the public, and it has no other users.

## What Jarvis accesses

When its owner signs in with Google, Jarvis can **read** (it cannot send, change or delete):

- **Google Calendar**: events, to answer questions about the owner's schedule and to add an event
  only after the owner says yes.
- **Gmail**: messages, to summarise the owner's email and to read bank alert emails for spending totals.

## How the data is used and stored

- Google data is used only to answer the owner's own questions and to send the owner reminders.
- It is processed on the owner's own machine by a local AI model. It is **not** sent to any AI company,
  advertiser or other third party, and it is **never sold or shared**.
- Copies kept by Jarvis (for example spending totals and search indexes) stay on the owner's machine
  and in the owner's own encrypted backups.

## Removing access

The owner can remove Jarvis's access at any time at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), and can delete Jarvis's
stored data by deleting its database and files.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.
