What it does

Everything you'd ask a personal assistant, with the facts kept exact: totals are counted by the database, never guessed by the AI.

💬

Chat

Ask anything, in English or your own language; it replies in the language you write in. Speak instead of typing.

📄

Your files

PDFs, Word files, notes and photos of documents. Answers name the file they came from.

📅

Calendar and tasks

Google and iCloud calendars, its own tasks with reminders. It adds an event only after you say yes.

💰

Money

Your bank's alert emails become exact spending by day, week, month and category, plus each card's bill.

💊

Health

Medicine reminders, and lab results with LOW/HIGH flags. It summarises; it doesn't diagnose.

👁️

Watches

A bus fare, movie bookings opening, or a price on any page: it checks and tells you when it finds it.

🔔

Briefings and renewals

A morning brief, a weekly summary, and reminders before insurance, rent or passports run out.

🎙️

Siri

“Hey Siri, Ask Jarvis”: ask from the lock screen or in the car, and Siri reads the answer.

Private by design

Your documents, mail and bank details stay on a machine you control.

  • The AI runs on your machine, in Ollama. Nothing is sent to an AI company, unless you choose a cloud AI yourself.
  • Read-only where it can be: Gmail and Drive are only read; Jarvis never sends email or pays anything.
  • A sign-in with a password or Google before anyone can open it, with guessing slowed down.
  • Encrypted backups before anything leaves the machine.
  • Instructions hidden in files and emails (“ignore previous instructions…”) are removed before the AI sees them.

Install it

The repository is the installer: copy it onto the machine that will run Jarvis, and run ./install.sh. It takes 15–30 minutes, mostly downloads.

☁️ A free cloud server

  • Any host: Oracle, any VPS, a home PC or a Mac.
  • Your country: number style, currency, time zone and languages are settings.

Honest limits

  • A small model makes mistakes, and the weekly tests show them openly.
  • Slower on a free server: 15–40 seconds for a short reply, minutes with tools; seconds on a Mac.
  • Money from alert emails only: cash isn't counted.
  • One person per install, and the phone app is a web app on your Home Screen.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. All the software is open source, and Oracle's Always Free server costs nothing within its limits. On your own Mac or PC it costs only electricity.

Does anything I say go to OpenAI, Google or anyone else?

No. The model runs on your machine. Google is contacted only to read your own calendar, mail and Drive with your sign-in, and Telegram only if you turn it on.

Which languages does it speak?

Whatever the model handles well, which includes most major languages. English, Tamil and Tanglish are tested every week.

Do I need to know servers or coding?

The guides go step by step, and an AI agent can do the setup with you. You'll still create the accounts and type your own passwords.

Is the demo real?

It's the real phone app running in your browser, on made-up data recorded from a real Jarvis. Chat answers are pre-written in the demo; nothing you do is saved or sent anywhere.
